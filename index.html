Project
Master's Thesis
Year
2024–2025
Role
Lead Engineer
Tools
MATLAB · ANSYS

The Problem

Industrial 6-DOF manipulators exhibit significant end-effector vibration during high-speed operations. Baseline: ±2.3 mm displacement vs ±0.5 mm target.

"The first bending mode at 12.4 Hz accounted for 68% of total displacement."

Approach

Modal analysis via ANSYS, LQR control design in MATLAB/Simulink, physical validation on scaled prototype. Piezoelectric actuators at joints 3 and 5 based on modal strain energy maps.

[ Figure: 1st bending mode @ 12.4 Hz — ANSYS ]

Results

34% reduction in peak displacement. Settling time 380 ms → 210 ms. Prototype confirmed within 6% margin.

Final: ±0.38 mm · 210 ms settling · within ±0.5 mm target
