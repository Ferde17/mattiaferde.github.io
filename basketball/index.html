Basketball career

Player.
Since 2009.

Seventeen seasons so far. I started with minibasket at Fortitudo and moved up one level at a time until my Serie A debut with Virtus in 2021. After that came Serie B with Castel San Pietro and, with Francesco Francia Zola, the play-off semifinals two seasons in a row.
