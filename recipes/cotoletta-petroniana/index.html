Cotoletta alla Petroniana

Bologna's version of the cutlet. Breaded veal topped with prosciutto and Parmigiano, finished in a little broth with the lid on.

Serves
4
Prep time
15m
Difficulty
Easy
Cost
€€
Total time
40m
Origin
Bologna

Ingredients

  • 4veal cutlets
  • 2eggs
  • As neededbreadcrumbs
  • 4 slicesprosciutto crudo
  • 120 gParmigiano Reggiano, shaved
  • 1 ladlemeat broth
  • As neededbutter

Method

  1. 1

    Flatten the cutlets, dip in beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs.

  2. 2

    Fry in butter until golden on both sides.

  3. 3

    Lay a slice of prosciutto and a layer of Parmigiano on each cutlet.

  4. 4

    Pour a ladle of broth into the pan, cover, and cook on low until the cheese melts, 4–5 minutes.

  5. 5

    Serve immediately, spooning the buttery broth over the top.

Notes · variations & storage

The traditional cut is veal. On a weeknight, pork or chicken works fine.
